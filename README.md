---

## How I think about AI systems

> **The model is only one component. The surrounding system must be reliable, observable, versioned, testable, and connected to reality.**

I prefer architectures where:

- AI proposes; deterministic systems verify
- human approval controls high-impact changes
- state is explicit and recoverable
- agents have bounded authority
- demos are separated from production readiness
- important artifacts are traceable to source, version, and decision
